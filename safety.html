Child Safety Standards — Friendy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

Friendy has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) of any kind. This page describes the standards and practices we follow to prevent CSAE, in line with Google Play's child safety standards policy.

How Friendy is designed to reduce risk

  • No stranger discovery. Friendy has no public profiles, no browsing of other users, and no matching or recommendation system. You can only send a question to someone whose phone number you already have — there is no way to find or contact a person you don't already know.
  • Phone-verified accounts. Every account is tied to a real, verified phone number via SMS one-time-password verification (Firebase Authentication). There are no anonymous or throwaway accounts.
  • Adult audience. Friendy is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.
  • User-controlled data. Any user can request full deletion of their account and all associated content (questions, answers, and profile data) at any time by contacting us.

Reporting a concern

If you believe Friendy is being used to harm a child, to exchange CSAE material, or to contact a minor inappropriately, contact us immediately at ranjithstar256@gmail.com with as much detail as possible (the phone number involved, screenshots, and a description of what happened). Reports are reviewed personally by the app's developer.

We will remove offending content and accounts, cooperate with law enforcement, and report apparent CSAE to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or the equivalent authority, as required by law.
